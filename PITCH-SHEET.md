# PITCH SHEET — netra.news

**REPORTER:** Al Amin Tusher — tusher@netra.news

**WORKING TITLE:** The ৳3,723 Crore Blind Spot — 645 Winning Firms Named, Not One Owner

**LOGLINE:** Six government development authorities awarded ৳3,723.7 crore across 1,158 e-GP tenders while leaving the one field that would make the money traceable — who owns the winning company — blank in every single record.

**LOQ (Line of Questioning)**

1. Why is beneficial ownership empty in all 1,158 records when ITT 5.14 obliges the winner to file it and ITT 68.1 obliges the procuring entity to publish it on the BPPA portal for 30 days — and 561 of 645 awards clear the BDT 10 lakh disclosure floor?
2. In the 201 of 591 tenders where only one bid survived evaluation, did the Tender Evaluation Committee consider rejection under ITT 56.2(b), and what did the Head of the Procuring Entity decide?
3. Why were 130 contracts signed on exactly day 28 after the NOA — 124 of them RAJUK's — and none at all on days 29–37, when Rule 123(9) of the PPR 2025 permits 28 days only above BDT 250 million, a tier only 24 of 645 awards reach?
4. How did Spectra Engineers Ltd. come to hold 28.7% of all awarded value on four CDA tenders, and why was its ৳881.24 crore package tendered nationally, drawing two bids?
5. Why is `Estimated_Cost` blank in every record, when ITT 56.2(a) makes the official cost estimate the legal test for rejecting an overpriced tender?
6. Why were 52 contracts the portal itself flags as signed late — including 13 signed 150 days after NOA, all to M/S. Sany Construction, all from the Office of the Chief Engineer, all on 2019-12-25 — signed at all, when ITT 67.3 makes that grounds for annulment and forfeiture of tender security?

**SYNOPSIS**

3,436 tender documents sold, 2,749 bids received, 1,752 ruled responsive, 591 contracts awarded across 1,158 tenders worth ৳3,723.7 crore, by RAJUK, CDA, CoxDA, KDA, RDA and GDA. The register is complete enough to move the money and too incomplete to audit it.

Four patterns and two absences drive the story. Competition collapses: 201 of 591 awards (34%) had a single responsive bidder, worst at the smallest authorities — RDA 60.7%, CoxDA 56.2%, CDA 52.1% — and in 53 tenders three or more rivals became one (ITT 56.2(b), "lack of effective competition"). Money concentrates: Spectra Engineers Ltd. holds ৳1,068 crore, 28.7% of all value, on four CDA tenders; three CDA officers routed 86–99% of their approved value to it. Timing clusters: 130 signings on day 28, zero on days 29–37, 124 of them RAJUK's (ITT 67.2 read with PPR 2025 Rule 123(9)). Relationships repeat: 98 contractor–office pairings recur, two of them 14 times (ITT 4.2(c), collusive practice).

The two absences are new and, I think, the actual lede. `Beneficial_Owner`, `Ownership_Percentage` and `Owner_Country` are empty in all 1,158 records, so common control — the thing clause 4.2(c) and 5.13 exist to catch — cannot be tested from the public record at all. And `Estimated_Cost` is blank throughout, with `Performance_Security_Provided_On_Time` blank in all 645 awards, disabling the ITT 56.2(a) price benchmark and the ITT 63.2 security check.

Framing note: these are red flags requiring answers, not proven wrongdoing. Two citation caveats are carried openly — the contracts run 2019–2025, largely under the PPR 2008 regime, so PPR 2025 clause numbers need their 2008 equivalents before publication; and JICA Section 2.02 ("Size of Contract") is used as an international benchmark, not a binding rule, since only one tender in the set is JICA-funded.

**RIGHT TO REPLY & NEXT STEPS**

Responses already on record are themselves the story: authorities deny data was hidden, contractors blame the e-GP software for the invisible ownership fields, and government engineers describe openly published e-GP documents as "secret". Next: the 53 mass-disqualification evaluation reports, APP estimates via RTI, CDA on the Spectra concentration, RAJUK on day 28, and BPPA on why the beneficial-ownership publication requirement produced nothing.
