Signed on the last legal day

Six government development authorities awarded ৳3,723.7 crore of construction work through Bangladesh's electronic tender system. A record-by-record reading of 1,158 tenders finds competition collapsing to a single bidder, the same offices handing repeat work to the same contractors, more than a quarter of all money going to one firm — and one in five contracts signed on the very last day the law allows.
