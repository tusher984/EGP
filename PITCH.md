# PITCH — English

**Working Headline:** *The ৳3,723 Crore Blind Spot: 645 Winning Firms Named, Not One Owner*

**The Hook:** 3,436 tender documents sold, 2,749 bids received, 1,752 ruled responsive — and ৳3,723.7 crore awarded across 1,158 tenders by six development authorities. The record is complete enough to move the money, too incomplete to audit it.

**Evidence & Rule Violations**

- **Competition collapses.** 201 of 591 awards (34%) had one responsive bidder; in 53, three-plus rivals became one. Worst at the smallest authorities: RDA 60.7%, CoxDA 56.2%, CDA 52.1%. → *PPR 2025 ITT 56.2(b), "lack of effective competition."*
- **Concentration.** Spectra Engineers Ltd. holds ৳1,068 crore — 28.7% of all value — over four CDA tenders; three CDA officers sent it 86–99% of their books. Its ৳881 crore contract, tendered nationally, drew two bidders. → *JICA Section 2.02: lots should be "large enough to attract bids on an international basis."*
- **The 28-day cliff.** 130 contracts (20.2%) were signed on exactly day 28; days 29–37 zero; 124 are RAJUK's. → *ITT 67.2 with PPR 2025 Rule 123(9) — which caps most of these at 14 or 21 days.*
- **Repeat pairs.** 98 contractor–office pairings recur, two 14 times each. → *ITT 4.2(c), collusive practice.*
- **NEW — the ownership blackout.** `Beneficial_Owner`, `Ownership_Percentage`, `Owner_Country`: empty in all 1,158 records, though 561 awards clear the ৳10 lakh floor. → *ITT 5.14 and 68.1 require publishing the winner's beneficial ownership.* Common control is untestable.
- **NEW — no benchmark price.** `Estimated_Cost` is blank in all 1,158 records and `Performance_Security_Provided_On_Time` in all 645. → *ITT 56.2(a), 63.2.*

**Right to Reply & Next Steps:** Officials deny hiding data, contractors blame the e-GP software for missing ownership fields, engineers call published e-GP files "secret". The denials are the story. Next: the 53 evaluation reports, APP estimates, CDA on Spectra.
