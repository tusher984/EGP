Signed on the last legal day

Six government development authorities, and the smaller bodies attached to them, awarded ৳3,724 crore of construction work through Bangladesh's electronic tender system. A record-by-record reading of 1,158 tenders finds competition collapsing to a single bidder, the same offices handing repeat work to the same contractors, and one in five contracts signed on the very last day the law allows — a pattern of red flags that warrants scrutiny.
