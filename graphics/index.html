How to read these figures

Every number below is a count drawn from public tender notices and contract-award records. Concentration, single-responsive bidding and a cluster of awards on the last statutory day are red flags that warrant scrutiny — not, on their own, evidence of wrongdoing. Names appear only because the public record names them.

Method & limitations

Compiled from 1,155 tender notices and 645 award PDFs. Bid counts exist for 591 tenders; officer aggregates cover 73 named officers across 11 units. Chattogram is recorded under two portal spellings, kept distinct except where a single place is required. The cartogram is schematic, not a projection.
